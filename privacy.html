Hyperdrive privacy policy

Hyperdrive, an IGLO Games production. Last updated 24 September 2026.

Hyperdrive is a pinball game. It collects as little as it can: only what the online leaderboards and the online backup of your progress need to work. It has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask for an account, an email address or any other personal details.

What is sent to the online leaderboard

When a game ends with a score good enough for the leaderboard, the game sends it to LootLocker, the service that runs the leaderboard. A daily challenge game that beats your best that day goes to the day's own board in the same way. It sends:

WhatWhy
The name you typed in (up to 8 letters)So the score has a name on the board
Your score, and the highest level that game reachedThis is the leaderboard
How many seconds into the game each level was first reachedSo other players can race your climb in the ghost race
For a daily challenge, which day it wasSo only that day's scores are shown
A random code the game made up the first time it ran, such as hd-3fa91c07… So the game can pick out your own scores on the board. It is not linked to your phone, your account or anything else about you, and it changes if you reinstall the game.

Like any website, LootLocker's servers also see the internet address your device connects from. Their handling of it is covered by LootLocker's own privacy policy.

The leaderboards are public: anyone playing the game can see the names, scores and levels on them. Please use a nickname, not your real name.

Your online backup

So that your progress is not lost with your phone, the game also keeps a copy of it with LootLocker: your trophies, your daily challenge streak and today's best daily score, your best game's score and climb, and the name you entered. It is stored under a player code of eight letters and numbers, such as HNFF-KYRJ, which the game made up and shows on the trophy page. Typing that code into the game on another phone or browser brings the progress over.

The code is not linked to your phone, an account or anything else about you. Anyone who has your code can load your progress into their game, so keep it to yourself. To have your backup deleted, email us your code.

What stays on your device

The game saves your own best scores, the name you last entered, whether the sound is muted, your trophies and daily streak, and the codes above. These stay on your device and are removed if you uninstall the game (or clear the site's data, for the web version).

On phones, the game reads the motion sensor so that shaking the phone nudges the table. That is used only while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for internet access, which the leaderboards and the online backup need. The game still works without a connection, using the scores saved on your device.

Children

Hyperdrive has no adverts and does not ask for personal information. The only thing a player types in is a name for the leaderboard. If a child has entered their real name and you would like it removed, please get in touch.

Removing your scores or backup

To have a name or score removed from a leaderboard, email hello@iglogames.com with the name and roughly when the game was played. To have your online backup deleted, send your player code from the trophy page.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything else, this page will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.
